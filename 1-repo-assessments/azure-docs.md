# Azure Cost Management and Billing documentation case study

## Portfolio summary

I owned and maintained Azure Cost Management and Billing documentation across cost analysis, budgets, alerts, invoices, pricing, billing accounts, APIs, cost allocation, and FinOps workflows. The work combined product documentation, cross-service integration, customer education, and long-term information architecture.

## What this demonstrates

- I became the documentation owner for a complex cloud-finance product area with many customer roles and billing models.
- I connected conceptual guidance with operational tasks such as estimating costs, monitoring spending, reviewing invoices, and integrating billing data.
- I translated product and stakeholder input into coherent customer guidance across Microsoft Azure documentation and Microsoft Learn content.
- I helped explain how Cost Management and Billing fit together and how FinOps practices support cloud-cost decisions.

## Strong examples to develop further

The strongest portfolio example is the end-to-end cost-management journey: estimate costs before deployment, monitor spending with budgets and alerts, analyze usage, review invoices, allocate costs, and integrate data through APIs and exports. A second example is the transition and handoff of Cost Management, Billing, and FinOps documentation to new owners while preserving product quality and publishing continuity.
