# System Center documentation case study

## Portfolio summary

I documented Microsoft systems-management products across successive generations, including Configuration Manager, Operations Manager, Service Manager, Capacity Planner, and related datacenter-management tooling. The public System Center documentation history preserves a visible record of that earlier work.

## What this demonstrates

- I built product knowledge across long-lived enterprise platforms and successive releases.
- I documented administrative workflows, deployment concepts, troubleshooting paths, and operational tooling for technical audiences.
- I maintained continuity through product transitions, changing terminology, and evolving management models.
