## Microsoft Learn: Azure cost management

I wrote all the modules in the Microsoft Learn path *Control Azure spending and manage bills*: six modules and 46 units on managing Azure costs and bills. The module linked below shows the approach: a beginner sequence with hands-on exercises and an assessment, so a learner finishes having done the task and checked their understanding.

I tracked completion rates and assessment scores, reported results to management, and revised the modules that performed poorly until completion improved. When a module's customer satisfaction fell in a given month, I assessed it, found the root cause, and made changes to improve it. Every module I was responsible for exceeded organizational OKRs. Management described the Cost Management content set as performing well with customers and high on quality scores. I also kept the content current as the product changed, updating modules for new features and responding to customer feedback, including GitHub issues and feedback submitted through Microsoft Learn. Related work covered partner Cost Management, Power BI cost analysis, and FinOps metadata.

You can read one of the modules in its published form: [Analyze costs and create budgets](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/analyze-costs-create-budgets-azure-cost-management/), also available as a [PDF writing sample](../samples/microsoft-learn-cost-management-writing-sample.pdf).

## Classroom training: U.S. Air Force, 1996 to 2000

As a staff systems analyst and trainer at Brooks Air Force Base, Texas, I trained users on the Defense Medical Logistics Standard Support system as it was deployed to Air Force health care facilities. The work covered the full training cycle:

- **Lesson design.** I helped develop lesson plans for end users and for trainers, with learning objectives, a basic-to-advanced sequence, exercises, hands-on labs, assessments, instructor guides, slide decks, quick-reference guides, and handouts, and revised them based on learner feedback.
- **Delivery.** I taught live classes and gave one-on-one support as part of a team of trainers, using each site's own data so learners practiced on their real workflows.
- **Reach.** Training reached about 15 hospitals and roughly 500 users.
- **Measurement.** I tracked training status, rosters, and pass and fail results in Excel and reported training metrics to facility management.
- **Reusable training.** I built a computer-based medical-logistics training program that the Department of Defense later adopted as its baseline for medical logistics training.
