## Evidence boundary

Public repository history and published documentation support externally verifiable claims. Career facts that depend on internal metrics, historical employment records, or non-public training records are labeled and presented as professional background rather than GitHub-verifiable evidence.

## Claims to keep private

Keep private repository URLs, internal work-item links, reviewer names, support-only diagnostics, staffing details, unpublished product decisions, and internal source documents out of application materials.
