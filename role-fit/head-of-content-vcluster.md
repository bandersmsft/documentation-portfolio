# Role fit: Head of Content, vCluster Labs

This page maps my evidence to the requirements in the vCluster Labs Head of Content posting. It marks each requirement as a strong match, a partial match, or not supported. I wrote it so the hiring team does not have to infer any of this.

The rest of this portfolio is written for general use. This page is the only vCluster-specific material in it.

## Summary

The role sits between Product Marketing and Demand Generation and owns a content engine for technical buyers in AI infrastructure. My strongest overlap is the content engine itself: strategy, editorial prioritization, technical writing for operators, subject matter expert orchestration, search and answer-engine readiness, and content quality measurement. My clearest gap is the demand-generation half: pipeline attribution, webinars, customer stories, research reports, and executive bylines.

I am a content leader with deep infrastructure fluency and production AI practice, not a demand-generation marketer. If the role needs the content engine built and the campaign machinery is being built alongside it, that fits. If it needs proven pipeline attribution on day one, it does not.

## What I would do first

This is method, not a claim of past results.

**Audit before planning.** Establish what exists, what ranks, what gets retrieved by AI answering systems, and what contradicts itself. I would run the retrieval side as question-driven evaluation against the questions buyers actually ask, because ranking position does not tell you whether a model can answer from your content.

**Fix the structural blockers before writing new material.** Chunk autonomy, heading hierarchy, disambiguation between similarly named concepts, and context completeness determine whether anything new gets retrieved at all.

**Set baselines I can defend.** Organic, AI referral, and conversion baselines first, so later claims about content influence rest on a measured starting point rather than on a narrative.

**Build the subject matter expert pipeline early.** The bottleneck in technical content is rarely writing capacity. It is access to engineers and a process that respects their time. An intake path and a predictable review rhythm are worth more than a larger editorial calendar.

## Honest assessment

The posting asks for a strategist who writes, deep technical credibility, production AI practice, and a search and answer-engine practice built from the ground up. I match those four well, and the answer-engine half of search is genuinely current for me rather than aspirational.

The posting also asks for pipeline impact, webinars, customer stories, an annual research report, executive bylines, and fluency with the standard marketing search stack. I have none of those. I can learn the tooling quickly, and the program-building experience transfers, but I am not going to present method as track record.
