# Portfolio role positioning

## Documentation leadership

Lead with Azure Cost Management and Billing, Azure Monitor, and System Center. Emphasize product ownership, information architecture, cross-functional alignment, customer impact, and long-term content stewardship.

Suggested positioning: “Senior technical writer and documentation lead who turns complex cloud and enterprise platforms into coherent customer journeys, maintainable information architectures, and reliable publishing systems.”

## Documentation engineering

Lead with Azure Monitor, FinOps toolkit, and Azure Compute. Emphasize Git-based workflows, structured content, metadata and navigation quality, troubleshooting design, automation, and documentation-as-code practices.

Suggested positioning: “Documentation engineer who combines technical writing, Git-based content systems, information architecture, and automation to improve the quality and maintainability of developer documentation.”

## AI and agent operations

Lead with Azure Monitor automation, FinOps toolkit, and the AI-assisted workflow evidence. Emphasize evaluation, screenshot freshness, retrieval quality, safe automation boundaries, and human review of generated content.

Suggested positioning: “Technical content systems builder who applies AI and automation to documentation workflows while preserving evidence, accuracy, reviewability, and customer trust.”

## Selection rule

Use three flagship pieces in an application. Add a fourth only when it matches the role. Keep the public evidence links visible, and keep internal corroboration out of the shared version.
