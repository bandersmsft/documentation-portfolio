# Portfolio role positioning

The portfolio supports three primary lanes: technical documentation, documentation/content systems, and technical learning/customer education. Choose the lane that best matches the role rather than trying to present every capability at equal weight.

## Technical writing and documentation leadership

Lead with Azure Cost Management and Billing, Azure Monitor, and System Center. Emphasize product ownership, information architecture, cross-functional alignment, customer and support signals, technical accuracy, and long-term content stewardship.

Suggested positioning: “Senior technical writer and content developer who turns complex cloud and enterprise platforms into coherent customer journeys, maintainable information architectures, and accurate customer guidance.”

## Documentation systems and engineering

Lead with Azure Monitor automation, the FinOps toolkit, and the AI-assisted workflow evidence. Emphasize Git-based workflows, structured content, metadata and navigation quality, automation, validation, screenshot freshness, retrieval quality, and human review.

Suggested positioning: “Documentation systems builder who combines technical writing, Git-based content engineering, AI-assisted workflows, and automation to improve the quality, maintainability, and reliability of technical content.”

## Technical learning and customer education

Lead with Microsoft Learn, Cost Management learning content, and the Air Force DMLSS training experience. Emphasize learning objectives, structured learning paths, hands-on exercises, assessments, localization, instructional media, completion-rate analysis, learner feedback, classroom delivery, and technical enablement.

Suggested positioning: “Technical learning and customer-education professional who translates complex software and operational workflows into structured learning experiences, hands-on practice, assessments, and measurable learner improvement.”

## AI and agent-enabled content work

Use this as a specialization within the documentation-systems lane when a role emphasizes generative AI, agents, retrieval, developer tooling, or workflow automation. Highlight reviewability and evidence rather than presenting AI as a replacement for technical judgment.

Suggested positioning: “Technical content systems builder who applies AI and automation to documentation workflows while preserving evidence, accuracy, reviewability, and customer trust.”

## Selection rule

Use three flagship pieces in an application. Add a fourth only when it directly matches the role. Keep public evidence links visible, distinguish public proof from professional-background claims, and keep internal corroboration out of shared materials.
