# Bill Anderson: technical documentation, content systems, and technical learning portfolio

I spent 26 years at Microsoft building documentation, content systems, and technical learning for cloud finance, observability, systems management, APIs, security, and customer education. I owned major content sets, used customer and product signals to improve content, and built automation that refreshes, verifies, and ships technical content with human review. Earlier, in the U.S. Air Force, I combined systems analysis, technical training, implementation, and medical-logistics support.

### Documentation engineering and artificial intelligence automation

I designed autonomous documentation workflows that produce reviewable draft pull requests. The work includes schema-validated result envelopes, admission control, SHA-256 provenance pinning, a 117-assertion release gate, browser automation against the live Azure portal, customer-identifier sanitization, screenshot-freshness automation, and artificial intelligence retrievability evaluation. A browser capture pipeline renders annotated output in roughly 12 seconds.

I also authored a VS Code extension, 50 packaged agent skills, and Model Context Protocol integrations, and used generative-AI-, LLM-, and agent-assisted workflows for content creation, review, visual-asset and screenshot workflows, and quality checks.

### Microsoft Learn, customer education, and learning experience design

I designed, authored, maintained, measured, and improved technical learning experiences. Across Cost Management and related topics, I contributed 31 merged pull requests to Microsoft Learn training and owned about five modules. I worked from learning objectives and structured learning paths through hands-on exercises, assessments, instructional media, localization, launch, measurement, and iterative improvement.

A representative example is [Introduction to analyzing costs and creating budgets with Microsoft Cost Management](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/analyze-costs-create-budgets-azure-cost-management/), a seven-unit beginner module with hands-on exercises and assessment that was localized into 17 languages. I also created instructional scripts, videos, and supporting learning assets and used completion-rate data and learner feedback to improve lower-performing modules.

### U.S. Air Force systems analysis, DMLSS implementation, and training

Before Microsoft, I worked in U.S. Air Force medical logistics and later as a systems analyst and trainer supporting the Defense Medical Logistics Standard Support system. I helped design training for end users and trainers, including learning objectives, lesson sequencing, exercises, hands-on labs, assessments, instructor guides, PowerPoint decks, quick-reference guides, and handouts.

I supported DMLSS implementations at about 15 Air Force health care facilities and trained approximately 500 users. I also built a reusable computer-based medical-logistics training program that was later adopted as the Department of Defense baseline for medical logistics training.
